MusicBridge Institutional Piano Estimate
=========================================
From: Sarah Martinez, Program Director
Date: Last week

Based on outreach to schools, churches, and performance venues:

Institutional pianos (US total):
- Schools (K-12 + college): ~1.5M pianos
- Churches: ~0.3M
- Concert halls, theaters, studios: ~0.2M
- **Total institutional: 2.0M pianos**

Tuning frequency for institutional pianos:
- Average: **2 tunings per year**
- Performance venues tune more frequently (quarterly), but this average reflects the mix.

Annual institutional tunings: 2.0M × 2 = **4.0M tunings/year**
