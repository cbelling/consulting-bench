DataLoop — Finance Retention Model (Exhibit B)
============================================
Starting ARR: $100.0M

Assumptions:
- Gross churn: 12%
- Expansion: 18%
- NDR = (1 − 12%) × (1 + 18%) = 0.88 × 1.18 = **103.8%**
- Net ARR from existing: $100M × 3.8% = **+$3.8M**

Both miss NDR 120% hurdle and Finance misses $12M net ARR.
