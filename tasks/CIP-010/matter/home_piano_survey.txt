MusicBridge Home Piano Survey Results
======================================
Survey date: Q2 2024
Sample: 2,400 US households with pianos

Key findings:
- Estimated US pianos in homes: **10.0 million**
- Piano owners who tune ≥1 time per year: 40%
- Among those who tune, average tunings per year: 1.2
- Piano owners who never tune: 60%

Note: Survey validation callback suggests 1.2 avg may be LOW — piano teachers in sample reported 2.0 tunings/year but represent only 8% of tuning households.
