Chemora US Lithium Processing — Exhibit A: Consultant Market View
==================================================================
From: McKenna Strategy Group

**US lithium processing demand (2028E): 200,000 tonnes**

Chemora realistic market share: **10%** = 20,000 tonnes annually

Unit economics (consultant view):
- Processing margin: **$3,000 per tonne**
- Annual EBITDA at 10% share: 20,000 × $3,000 = **$60 million**

Consultant recommendation: "Attractive market with $60M EBITDA clearing ~7% CapEx return threshold."

Note: Consultant margin assumes industry-standard 35% process efficiency and $8/lb carbonate pricing.
