DimpleForge Market Research — Golf Ball Loss Survey A (Exhibit A)
==================================================================
Survey date: Summer 2024
Sample: 1,200 US golfers across 50 courses

**Average golf balls lost per 18-hole course round: 1.2 balls**

Breakdown by handicap:
- High handicap (20+): 2.4 balls/round
- Mid handicap (10–20): 1.0 balls/round
- Low handicap (<10): 0.3 balls/round

Weighted average across all golfers: **1.2 balls per course round**

With 400M course rounds per year:
→ Estimated course loss: **480 million balls per year**
