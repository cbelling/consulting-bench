Range Ball Purchases & Consumption Data
========================================
From: Golf Range Operators Association

Annual US range ball purchases: **800 million balls per year**

Range ball lifecycle:
- Purchased balls used at driving ranges: 800M
- Balls that wear out / destroyed annually: **70% = 560 million balls**
- Balls recycled back into range rotation: 30%

**Consumed/destroyed range balls: 560 million per year**

Note: These balls are not "lost" on courses but are consumed through range use wear-and-tear (cracking, cuts, cover damage). They represent replacement demand separate from course loss.
